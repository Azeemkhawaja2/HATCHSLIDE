HATCHSLIDE privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

HATCHSLIDE does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The game works completely offline. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no in-app purchases and asks for no permissions.

Your stars, unlocks, settings, statistics and Daily Tide streak are saved only on this device. If you turn on Android backup for your device, Android may include this game data in your own device backup; HATCHSLIDE itself never sends it anywhere.

Uninstalling the game, or using Reset progress in Settings, deletes this data.

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact address on the game’s store page.
