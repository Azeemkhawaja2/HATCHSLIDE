## Full description

One swipe moves every hatchling on the beach. They all slide together until something stops them: a rock, a shell, a crab or each other. Get every baby sea turtle into the surf before the seagull swoops in.

HATCHSLIDE is a calm, clever sliding puzzle set on a hand-crafted 3D beach. Every level is small enough to see at a glance and deep enough to make you stop and think. Plan the order of your swipes, use one hatchling to stop another, and find the shortest way to the waves.

Five islands, 150 levels
Sunrise Cove, Coral Coast, Moonlit Bay, Crab Reef and Volcanic Isle each bring something new: sticky wet sand, shells that crack, ocean currents, rolling coconuts, sand pits, scuttling crabs, late eggs, swooping gull shadows and tide pools that carry you across the beach.

Daily Tide
A fresh puzzle every day of the year, with a streak to keep.

Turtle Journal
Meet seven sea turtle species, from the Green turtle to the Flatback, turn each one around in 3D and play as any you have met.

Made to be played your way

Fair and quiet
