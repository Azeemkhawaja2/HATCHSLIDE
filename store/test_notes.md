# Closed test notes

Plan and log for the closed test that new personal developer accounts must complete before production: at least 12 testers opted in for the last 14 continuous days. The production access form asks what testers reported and what changed; keep this file up to date and copy from it.

## Plan

1. Internal testing: upload the first AAB and install it on your own devices; run the checks below.
2. Closed testing: create a track, add at least 12 testers (an email list or Google Group), share the opt-in link and confirm that every tester has opted in. The 14 days count from the day the twelfth tester stays opted in.
3. Ship at least one small update during the 14 days, based on what testers report.
4. After day 14, apply for production access with the summary below.

## What to ask testers to try

- Play through Sunrise Cove (levels 1 to 30) and at least one later island.
- Play the Daily Tide on two different days.
- Rotate the device mid-level, fold or unfold a foldable, resize the window on a tablet or Chromebook, and leave the game and come back.
- Turn on reduced motion, colour-blind tiles and high contrast; try the arrow pad and the left-handed HUD.
- Try a keyboard or gamepad if they have one.
- Report anything that feels unfair, confusing, slow or broken, with the level number.
